WHAT THE DISCREPANCY WAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
pretx_inflammatory_report.txt section 3, written on 2026-08-29, counted the
NF-kB per-cell-type enrichment from this module's own live run and said

    "Positive enrichment in 6/13 cell types before treatment (1 at q < 0.05)
     and in 12/13 after (4 at q < 0.05)."

Supplementary Figure S10 panel C had been redrawn on 2026-09-03 from the table
the project adopted that day,

    13_R1.8_Neutrophil_Rebuilt_Recompute/outputs/nfkb_per_celltype_sound13.csv

whose t-test rows give 5 of 13 positive before treatment with 3 at q < 0.05,
and 12 of 13 after with 3 at q < 0.05. So the report and its own figure
disagreed.

HOW IT WAS RESOLVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The report is now rendered from the adopted table, in the same way and by the
same call that draws the panel. main() already ended with

    _panel_nfkb_shift(load_adopted_shift())

and it now also renders section 3 from load_adopted_shift(), so the text and
the panel are produced from one frame and cannot diverge again.

The constraint that stopped this on 2026-09-03 was respected rather than
lifted. Re-running main() would reread MoMac.h5ad and rewrite five outputs,
which would put a second set of contents behind already-cited filenames - the
nfkb_rankings_13types_mast.csv defect. So pretreatment_inflammatory.py gained

    --rewrite-report-from-adopted

the counterpart of the --redraw-adopted-panel flag added on 2026-09-03. It
rebuilds the report text alone: sections 1 and 2 are read back from this
module's own deposited pretx_state_abundance.csv and pretx_signature_scores
.csv, section 3 from the adopted table. MoMac.h5ad is never opened and no
analysis table is written.
