CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The IL-1b+ state does not distinguish responders from non-responders
   before treatment (P = 0.886); after treatment it separates in
   the direction of non-response without reaching significance at these
   group sizes (P = 0.329). NF-kB enrichment follows the same
   timing: positive in 5 of 13 cell types before treatment and
   3 at q < 0.05, against 12 of 13 and 3 after, with
   monocytes/macrophages the strongest
   population after treatment. The programme is therefore best described
   as emerging on treatment rather than as a pre-existing feature of the
   non-responder microenvironment.
