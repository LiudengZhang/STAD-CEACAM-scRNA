CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The IL-1b+ state does not separate responders from non-responders
   before treatment (P = 0.886); the separation appears after
   treatment (P = 0.329). Combined with the myeloid NF-kB
   enrichment reversing sign between the two timepoints, the programme
   is best described as emerging on treatment rather than as a
   pre-existing feature of the non-responder microenvironment.
