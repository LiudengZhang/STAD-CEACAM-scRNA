WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Does: the NF-kB transcription factors themselves show elevated
  inferred activity, measured from motif-supported direct targets, and
  the pathway's own negative-feedback programme (NFKBIA/IkB-alpha,
  TNFAIP3/A20, NFKB2, RELB) is induced. Feedback genes are transcribed
  only after nuclear translocation, so their induction is a specific
  footprint of activation rather than of generic inflammation.
  Does not: measure p65 phosphorylation, nuclear localisation, or IkB
  protein. The manuscript states this.
