SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS: THE SAME COMPARISON UNDER MAST
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Both methods were run on the same cells, the same genes and the same
  contrast; only the test differs. MAST fits ~ condition + sample_id +
  cngeneson, and with four patients per arm the condition term is close
  to collinear with sample_id, so the two are not mutually confirmatory.
  The manuscript therefore reports the t-test throughout.
