4. WHAT CANNOT BE CONTROLLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GSE251950 provides no annotation for Lauren classification, mucinous
  differentiation, or tumour architecture, and no spatial data exist for
  the chemo-immunotherapy cohort itself. Adjustment for epithelial
  content and density therefore addresses only part of the reviewer's
  concern. The manuscript claim is reduced accordingly, from CEACAM
  driving immune exclusion to CEACAM-high regions co-localising with an
  immune-excluded tissue architecture.
