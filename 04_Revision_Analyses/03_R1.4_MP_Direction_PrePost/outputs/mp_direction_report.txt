  The published test contrasts PRE-TREATMENT RESPONDERS against every
  other group. MP4 is LOWER in pre-treatment responders, i.e. HIGHER in
  non-responders. The manuscript sentence names non-responders and is
  therefore inverted. The reviewer's reading of the figure is correct.

  Corrected wording:
     "MP4 was significantly depleted in pre-treatment responders relative
      to all other groups (two-sided exact permutation P = 0.083), that is,
      enriched in non-responders, consistent with the CEACAM5/6 result."
